Example 2: Letters: "EINR", Pattern: "..T.." → finds words with T in 3rd position

Example 4: Letters: "ABC.." → finds words using ABC and 2 blank tiles

How it works

  • Enter your available letters (your rack)
  • Use dots (.) in your letters to represent blank tiles (wildcards)
  • Optionally enter a pattern with fixed letters on the board
  • In patterns: dots (.) represent empty spaces you'll fill
  • Use asterisk (*) in patterns to match any number of spaces (e.g., C*T matches CAT, CART, CABINET)
  • Each word shows its Scrabble score (without bonus squares)
  • Toggle between sorting by word length or by score
  • Uses a dictionary of 370,000+ English words
